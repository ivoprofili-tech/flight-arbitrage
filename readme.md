# Flight Arbitrage Finder

A tool for finding hidden-city (skiplagging) flight deals by searching for flights where your true destination appears as a layover.

## What is Skiplagging?

Skiplagging (also called hidden-city ticketing) is when you book a flight with a connection, but get off at the layover city instead of continuing to the final destination. Sometimes this is significantly cheaper than booking a direct flight to the layover city.

**Example:**
- You want to fly JFK → Denver (DEN)
- A direct flight costs $400
- But JFK → Los Angeles (LAX) with a layover in Denver costs $250
- You book the LAX flight but get off in Denver, saving $150
